FAQ

Common questions.

How do the tender reminders work?

We post tenders with their closing dates. Save a tender or add it to your calendar for alerts 3 days and 1 day before it closes, or message us on WhatsApp to have matching tenders sent to you.

I’ve never tendered. Can you help?

Yes. Beginners are exactly who we serve. We explain each step and guide you through it.

Do you guarantee I’ll win a tender?

No one can honestly guarantee that. We make sure you’re registered, eligible and well presented, which improves your chances.

Do you cover tenders advertised on site?

Yes. We handle both online and on-site published tenders.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the service. Book a free call and we’ll give you a clear quote.
